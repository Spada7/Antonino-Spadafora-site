Selected work

Thermal and fluid-dynamic problems I've actually solved, from hypersonic vehicles to liquid rocket engines.

MSc thesis · MBDA Italia

Reduced-order thermal model for hypersonic vehicles

A MATLAB model that returns full surface heat-flux and wall-temperature fields in seconds, where high-fidelity CFD takes hours — so thermal trade studies happen at the concept stage instead of after the design freezes.

  • —Real-gas chemical-equilibrium closure: five-species equilibrium air (N₂, O₂, NO, N, O) with Blottner viscosity and Wilke’s mixing rule
  • —Stagnation-point heat flux from Fay–Riddell, then heat-flux and wall-temperature distributions over the whole geometry
  • —Validated against Ansys Fluent reference cases to define its accuracy envelope
MATLAB Reduced-order modelling Ansys Fluent Real gas
PoliTo Rocket Team · 2023–2026

Efesto & VES — a liquid rocket engine and a flying rocket

Efesto — Propulsion & Thermal Engineer

Italy's first student-built regeneratively cooled liquid rocket engine. I ran the Star-CCM+ CFD of the injector–chamber coupling to characterise mixing and internal thermal loads, then coupled that thermal field into an Ansys Mechanical thermo-mechanical FEA of the chamber. The results drove material selection and flanged-joint sizing, and I designed the injection head and injector array in SOLIDWORKS. Coordinated a sub-team of 3–4 engineers.

VES — FEM Analyst

Linear and nonlinear FEA on load-bearing components and composite joints, with parametric material-selection and buckling studies on the CFRP body tube under combined axial and lateral loading. VES Mark I flew at the 2024 European Rocketry Challenge to a 3000 m apogee.

SOLIDWORKS · September 2025

Jet engine — full CAD assembly, from the nose cone to the nozzle

A complete jet engine modelled from scratch as a CAD exercise: intake fan and nose cone, three axial compressor sections, an annular combustion stage with its cans and fuel ports, a multi-stage turbine on the main shaft and bearings, and a variable-geometry exhaust nozzle whose petals are moved by lever linkages. The assembly has three configurations — closed, working and open — that switch the nozzle between convergent, cylindrical and flared. About 60 custom parts organised in nine sub-assemblies, fully mated, with the fasteners driven by circular patterns.

Publications

27th ESA PAC Symposium · Trondheim, Norway · 31 May – 4 June 2026

Design and Development of a N₂O/Ethanol Augmented Spark Igniter

L. Ruggieri, C. Avati, A. Morra, A. Sepe, A. Spadafora, M. Girotto, F. Zeloni, A. Improta — PoliTo Rocket Team, Politecnico di Torino

A fully bi-liquid torch igniter for Efesto, drawing the same N₂O/ethanol propellants as the 5 kN main engine — unlike the gas-fed igniters common in the open literature. The operating point (34.4 g/s, mixture ratio 14, 20 bar) came from an energy balance on the ignition kernel, chemical-equilibrium analysis and a statistical benchmark of existing hardware. The modular Inconel 718 heat-sink architecture was verified by transient thermal analysis over a 2.5 s firing.
